About

School Property Information System

A browser-based asset registry for recording property details, tracking accountable persons, monitoring item status, and generating QR codes for fast property verification.

Purpose

Inventory Control

Asset Records Maintain property numbers, serial numbers, acquisition data, and remarks.
Accountability Track assigned personnel and item status across the property list.
QR Support Generate, copy, download, and print QR data for each registered asset.
