About

School Property Information System

The School Property Information System (SPIS) is an integrated platform for organizing school property records and supporting day-to-day property accountability. It brings inventory information, personnel assignments, issuance documents, QR labels, and reporting tools together in one workspace to help custodians maintain consistent records and retrieve information efficiently.

This project was initiated by Janine Carlyle G. Tuwahan, Administrative Officer II.

Platform capabilities

Property inventory

Record and review school assets, including identifying, acquisition, location, and accountable-person details.

Personnel accountability

Maintain personnel records and review assigned properties, pending or issued ICS items, related documents, history, and activity.

QR asset labels

Create QR codes for property records and manage downloaded codes for convenient identification and verification.

Issuance documents

Prepare and manage Inventory Custodian Slips (ICS) and Reports of Semi-Expendable Property Issued (RSPI).

Dashboards and reports

View property totals, category and year breakdowns, maintenance requests, and accountable-person summaries; configure and generate reports.

School and system settings

Manage school details and access system preferences, including theme and database connection information.
